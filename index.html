macOS Storage reported 65 GB used by "Applications." The Applications folder itself held only 11 GB. No existing tool would have flagged it, let alone explained it. That gap — between what macOS reports and what you can actually find — is the whole reason SpaceWise exists.

Privacy, in plain terms

Only you and your Mac see this. No one else.

No account, no sign-in, no server SpaceWise talks to in the background. Scanning happens entirely on your machine — the app never reads what's inside your files, only their paths and sizes, and it never sends either of those anywhere.

You don't have to take our word for it: open Activity Monitor's Network tab, or a tool like Little Snitch, while SpaceWise scans and cleans. You'll see nothing leave your Mac.

How it works

Three steps, under 60 seconds

1

Scan

SpaceWise walks your home directory (or the full system, opt-in) and finds what's actually using your storage — including the folders Finder hides.

2

Understand

Every finding is matched against a rules database and explained in plain English, then grouped by category and safety rating. Even the storage SpaceWise can't individually list gets accounted for, not left as a mystery gap.

Clean safely

Select what you want gone. Safe items clean with one click; anything risky requires you to explicitly confirm before it's touched.
